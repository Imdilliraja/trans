About Tranquil Homes

A serene and comfortable stay in the heart of Bangalore

Tranquil Homes, owned by Mr. Prakash Palani, offers a serene and comfortable stay in the heart of Bangalore. Strategically located near prominent areas such as Ulsoor Lake, Kammanahalli, and Frazer Town, our accommodations provide guests with easy access to the city's vibrant culture and attractions.

Prime Locations

  • Ulsoor Lake: Just a short walk from our property, Ulsoor Lake offers picturesque views and a tranquil environment for morning walks and leisure activities.
  • Kammanahalli: Known as "Kamanhattan" for its cosmopolitan vibe, Kammanahalli is a melting pot of cultures, featuring diverse eateries, shopping centers, and entertainment options.
  • Frazer Town: A historic neighborhood celebrated for its colonial architecture and culinary delights, Frazer Town provides a rich cultural experience.

Accommodations and Amenities

Tranquil Homes offers fully furnished rooms designed for comfort and convenience. Our amenities include:

  • High-Speed Wi-Fi: Stay connected with our uninterrupted and unlimited internet access.
  • Regular Housekeeping: Our professional housekeeping team ensures a clean and tidy environment throughout your stay.
  • 24/7 Power Backup: Enjoy hassle-free operations with our reliable power backup system.
  • Entertainment Zones: Relax and unwind in our gaming and lounging areas, or participate in community events.
  • Safety Measures: Your security is our priority, with CCTV surveillance, biometric access, and regular sanitization protocols in place.

Dining Options

While we offer home-cooked meals to our guests, the surrounding neighborhoods boast a plethora of dining options. From local South Indian delicacies to international cuisines, there's something to satisfy every palate.

Guest Reviews

Our commitment to excellence is reflected in the feedback from our guests. Many have praised our prime location, cleanliness, and the friendly demeanor of our staff. We continuously strive to enhance our services to ensure a memorable stay for all.
