Terms & Conditions

Please read carefully before making your booking

Welcome to Tranquil Homes, where we strive to provide a comfortable and memorable stay for our guests. By making a reservation at our property, you agree to comply with the following terms and conditions.

Arrival and Departure Policy

  • Check-In Timings: 13:00 Hours Onwards. Early arrival is subject to availability. For guaranteed early check-in, a reservation must be made starting from the previous night.
  • Check-Out Timings: 10:00 – 12:00 Hours. Late check-out is chargeable and subject to availability and payment.
  • Outside food is not allowed on the premises.

Hotel Policy

  • A picture of the guest will be taken via the webcam at the reception for records at check-in.
  • Guests must provide their mobile number and official email address.
  • A valid government-issued photo ID (e.g., driving license, passport, voter ID) and a company business card or company ID card must be presented to confirm the booking.
  • The meal plan for the selected booking includes breakfast.
  • The primary guest checking in must be at least 18 years of age.
  • Children accompanying adults must be between 1-12 years old.
  • Extra beds provided will be either a folding cot or a mattress.
  • Bookings cannot be canceled on or after the check-in date and time mentioned in the Hotel Confirmation Voucher.
  • Early check-in or late check-out is subject to availability and may be chargeable by the hotel.
  • Standard check-in time is 12 PM, and standard check-out time is 12 PM.
  • The amount paid for the room does not include charges for optional services and facilities such as room service, mini bar, snacks, or telephone calls.
  • The hotel reserves the right of admission. Accommodation may be denied to guests posing as a couple if suitable proof of identification is not presented at check-in.
  • For any booking updates, the user shall pay applicable cancellation/modification charges.
  • Modified bookings are subject to availability and the revised booking policy of the hotel.
  • Visitors are not allowed.
  • Two-wheeler parking is not allowed. Four-wheeler parking is subject to availability and at the owner's risk.
  • The hotel is not responsible for any loss of valuables or personal belongings on the premises.

Cancellation Policy

  • Cancellation less than 72 hours before check-in: No refund.
  • Cancellation before 72 hours of check-in: 100% refund.
  • No-show: No refund.
  • Booking cannot be canceled or modified on or after the check-in date and time mentioned in the Hotel Confirmation Voucher.
